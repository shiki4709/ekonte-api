# Roadmap

The current release focuses on reusable reference structure, generation, and inspectable pacing. These are candidate directions, not delivery commitments.

## Evaluation before stronger claims

- Permissioned fixtures covering hard cuts, slides, talking heads, music, and silence.
- Human-reviewed shot/function labels and segmentation/alignment measurements.
- Explicit comparison of generation quality, constraint failures, processing time, and cost.

## Better narration constraints

- Language-aware counting and timing, especially for Japanese and Chinese.
- Optional forced alignment rather than evenly distributing words within segments.
- More controllable editorial policies for sensory and spectacle formats.

## Developer integrations

- Additional implementations of the provider interface.
- Export adapters for editing and production tools.
- Agent integrations built on the stable Analysis JSON contract.

## Hosted deployments

- Per-user ownership and authorization.
- Durable queue execution, cancellation, and safe retry semantics.
- Stronger isolation for untrusted media.
