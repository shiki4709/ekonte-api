# Community guidelines

Ekonte welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and discuss the work rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback. Assume good intent and make room for corrections.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Share only code, examples, and media you have permission to contribute.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant. Do not post spam, credentials, or confidential material.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project constructive. Follow GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content; do not publish private details in an issue. Security vulnerabilities should use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces.
